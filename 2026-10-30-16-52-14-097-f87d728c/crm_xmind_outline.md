# MY CRM (Custom Relationship & Business Brokerage System)

## 1. Homepage: Daily Command Center
### 1.1 Calls Hub
- Calls To Make (Scheduled / Outbound Queue)
- Calls Already Made (Instant Call Logger)
  - Date & Time Logged
  - Contact Name & Phone
  - Call Outcome (Spoke with contact, Left voicemail, No answer, Gatekeeper)
  - Conversation Notes & Next Action
- Call Status Views
  - Upcoming / Scheduled Calls
  - Missed / Overdue Calls
  - Completed Call History

### 1.2 Meetings Hub
- Upcoming Meetings (Today & This Week)
- Completed Meetings (With debrief notes)
- Missed / Rescheduled Meetings

### 1.3 Tasks & Follow-up Queue
- Upcoming Tasks (Due Today / Upcoming)
- Overdue / Missed Tasks
- Completed Tasks (Checklist history)

---

## 2. Business Account Fields (Companies)
- Record Obtained Date (Date lead/record acquired from Salesgenie, referral, etc.)
- Company / Business Name
- Business Phone Number
- Website URL
- Business Social Media Links (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube)
- Business Physical Address (Street, City, State, Zip)
- Annual Revenue (or Revenue Range, e.g., $1M–$2.5M)
- Number of Employees (or Employee Range, e.g., 5–10)
- Property / Real Estate Status:
  - Land & Building: Leased / Rented / Owned / N/A
  - Monthly Rent / Lease Expiration (if leased)
- Type of Business (Plain English: Plumbing, Auto Repair, Restaurant, Retail, etc.)
- Years in Business / Year Founded
- Primary Owner / Contact Link
- Listing Status (Not for sale, Thinking about selling, Active listing for sale)
- Interested Buyers Roster (With NDA status per buyer)
- General Business Notes

---

## 3. Contact Fields (People)
- Record Obtained Date (Date record obtained/met)
- First & Last Name
- Title / Role (Owner, President, Agent, Manager, etc.)
- Direct Phone / Cell Phone
- Email Address
- Personal Website URL
- Personal Social Media Links (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter)
- Mailing Address
- Category Labels / Tags (Select all that apply):
  - Personal / Friend / Family
  - Entertainment Field
  - Real Estate Agent (Commercial / Residential)
  - Vendor (Dentist, CPA, Plumber, Attorney, etc.)
  - Business Owner / Seller
  - Business Buyer / Investor
- Linked Business Account (Optional)
- NDA Status (Not Sent, Sent - Pending, Signed & Approved)
- Buyer Preferences (Target budget, liquid cash, industries of interest)
- Notes & Conversation History Log

---

## 4. NDA & Compliance Tracking
### 4.1 NDA Status Workflow
- 1. Not Sent
- 2. Sent - Awaiting Signature
- 3. Signed & Verified (Green Light to send Financials)
- 4. Rejected / Expired
### 4.2 NDA Records
- Signed Date Timestamp
- Attached PDF / Electronic Signature Link
- Linked Contact + Linked Business Listing
