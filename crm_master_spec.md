# MY CRM System (Master Specification & Xmind Map)

## 1. Homepage: Daily Command Center
### 1.1 Calls Hub
- Calls To Make (Scheduled Outbound Queue)
- Calls Already Made (Instant Call Logger)
  - Date & Time Logged
  - Contact Name & Phone
  - Call Outcome (Spoke with contact, Left voicemail, No answer, Gatekeeper)
  - Notes & Next Action
- Call Views
  - Upcoming / Scheduled Calls
  - Completed Call History
  - Missed Calls

### 1.2 Meetings Hub
- Upcoming Meetings (Today & This Week)
- Completed Meetings (With Debrief Notes)
- Missed / Rescheduled Meetings

### 1.3 Tasks & Reminders Hub
- Upcoming Tasks (Due Today / Upcoming)
- Completed Tasks Checklist
- Overdue / Missed Tasks

---

## 2. Business Accounts (Companies)
### 2.1 General Business Information
- Record Obtained Date
- Company / Business Name
- Business Phone Number
- Website URL
- Business Social Media Links (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube)

### 2.2 Physical Location (Address)
- Business Address — Street
- Business Address — City
- Business Address — State
- Business Address — Zip Code

### 2.3 Operations & Property
- Annual Revenue (or Revenue Range, e.g. $1M–$2.5M)
- Number of Employees (or Employee Range, e.g. 5–10)
- Property Status (Leased or Owned)
- Type of Business (e.g. Dental Practice, Plumbing, Auto Repair, Restaurant, Law Firm, Escrow Co)
- Years in Business / Year Founded

### 2.4 Relationships & Status
- Primary Owner / Contact (Linked to Contact Person)
- Listing Status (Not for sale, Thinking about selling, Active listing for sale, Deal Partner/Vendor)
- Interested Buyers Roster (Linked Buyers + NDA Status per Buyer)
- General Business Notes

---

## 3. Contacts (People)
### 3.1 Personal & Communication Details
- Record Obtained Date
- First Name
- Last Name
- Title / Role (Owner, President, Attorney, CPA, Escrow Officer, Real Estate Agent, etc.)
- Cell Phone
- Other Phone (Office, Home, Direct line, etc.)
- Email Address
- Personal Website URL
- Personal Social Media Links (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter)

### 3.2 Contact Address
- Contact Address — Street
- Contact Address — City
- Contact Address — State
- Contact Address — Zip Code

### 3.3 Category Labels & Tags (Multi-Select)
- Business Owner (Owns a dental practice, plumbing co, auto shop, restaurant, etc.)
- Business Buyer / Investor
- Deal Partner (Attorney, CPA, Escrow Officer, Title Company, SBA Lender)
- Real Estate Agent (Commercial / Residential)
- Entertainment Field
- Trades / Contractor
- Personal / Friend / Family

### 3.4 Business & Deal Connections
- Linked Business Account (Optional link to Company)
- NDA Status (None, Sent, Signed)
- Buyer Preferences (Target Budget, Liquid Cash, Target Industries)
- Notes & Past Conversations Log

---

## 4. NDA & Compliance Tracking
### 4.1 NDA Status Workflow
- 1. Not Sent
- 2. Sent - Awaiting Signature
- 3. Signed & Approved (Green light to disclose financials)
- 4. Rejected / Expired

### 4.2 NDA Record Fields
- Date Signed Timestamp
- PDF Document / Electronic Signature Link
- Linked Buyer Contact
- Linked Business Listing Account

---

## 5. Deals & Transaction Pipeline
### Stage 1: Initial Talk & Intake
- Buyer or Seller first conversation & qualification

### Stage 2: Valuation & Listing Agreement
- Financial review, recasting earnings & listing agreement signed

### Stage 3: Marketing & NDA Inquiries
- Buyer inquiry received, NDA executed, confidential package released

### Stage 4: Offers & LOI Negotiation
- Buyer tour, owner meeting, Letter of Intent (LOI) submitted & agreed

### Stage 5: In Escrow & Due Diligence
- Escrow opened, deposit wired, due diligence audit, lease transfer

### Stage 6: Closed Deal & Commission
- Bill of sale completed, funds dispersed, commission paid
